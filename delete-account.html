Delete Your DuelFit Account

Last updated: October 26, 2026

In the App

Open DuelFit, go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account and confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without the App

Email adminduelfit@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my DuelFit account" and your username. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What Is Deleted

  • Your account, email address and password
  • Your profile, username, full name and profile photo
  • Your challenges, forfeits, proof photos, videos and links, for you and for the other player
  • Your friendships, friend requests and invite record
  • Your push notification token

Nothing is kept after deletion, except copies in our database provider's backups, which are overwritten within a limited period. See the Privacy Policy for details.
