Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DuelFit mobile application (the "App") collects and uses your personal data, who can see it, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

The data controller is Simeon Glabadanidis, Sofia, Bulgaria, the individual who develops and runs DuelFit. For anything about your data, contact adminduelfit@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information — your full name, username, email address and password (stored only in encrypted form), and your confirmation that you are at least 16.
  • Profile photo — optional.
  • Challenge activity — the matches you pick, your predictions, challenge results, the forfeits you agree to, and whether each forfeit was delivered or missed.
  • Forfeit proof — photos or videos you choose to upload, and links you add (for example a Strava activity).
  • Friends — friend requests you send and accept.
  • Invite code — optional. If you sign up with someone's invite code, we record who invited you.
  • Reports and blocks — reports you send about other players, and the players you block. Reports others send about you are seen only by us.
  • Push notification token — a device token used to send you notifications about your challenges and friend requests.

We do not collect payment information, location data, your contacts, or any health or fitness data from your device. The App asks for access to your photo library or camera only when you choose to add a profile photo or a proof, and only uses the items you pick. We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers or analytics trackers.

3. Why We Use It and Our Legal Basis

  • To provide the App (your account, challenges, results, forfeits, proofs, friends and notifications) — necessary to perform our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the App safe (handling reports, blocking, preventing abuse, fixing errors, securing accounts) — our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To understand how people find DuelFit, using invite codes — our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal obligations — Art. 6(1)(c).

Giving us your account information is necessary to use the App. Your profile photo, proofs and invite code are optional. We do not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we make no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. What Other Users Can See

  • Any signed-in DuelFit user can see your username, profile photo and reliability (how many of your forfeits you delivered or missed), and can find you by searching your username.
  • Your full name is visible only to your accepted friends and to players you share a challenge with.
  • The other player in a challenge can see that challenge, its forfeit, and any proof you upload for it. Proof photos and videos are stored privately and are not accessible to anyone else.
  • Who invited whom is not shown to anyone in the App.

5. Where Your Data Is Stored and Who Processes It

Your data, including profile photos and proofs, is stored with Supabase on servers in the European Union. These providers process data on our behalf, only to run the App:

  • Supabase — database, file storage and authentication (EU).
  • Expo, together with Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — deliver push notifications. They receive your device token and the notification text. These providers are based in the United States; transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • TheSportsDB — provides match fixtures and results. We send it no personal data.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. How Long We Keep It

  • Your account, challenges, proofs and friendships are kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Your push token is replaced whenever you sign in on a new device and deleted with your account.
  • Backups held by our database provider are overwritten within a limited period after deletion.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy of it, including in a portable format;
  • have inaccurate data corrected (you can edit your username and full name in Settings);
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД), www.cpdp.bg.

To use these rights, use Settings > Request My Data in the App or email adminduelfit@gmail.com. We respond within one month.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, profile photo, challenges, proofs and friendships. Challenges you shared with another player are deleted for them too. If you cannot access the App, see how to request deletion.

9. Age Requirement

You must be at least 16 years old to use DuelFit, and you confirm this when you sign up. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes we will also let you know by email or in the App.
